## Audit conclusions by method

- **CIF:** implementation differences adjacent cumulative hazards at monthly boundaries, treats each as an integrated hazard on that unit interval, applies the update only when `entry < interval_end`, and safely handles total increment zero. The recursion is exact only for the assumed piecewise-constant within-month hazards. It differs from the discrete event/risk-set AJ update. Delayed-entry predictions are conditional on event-free status through entry.
- **Delayed entry:** code uses left-truncated risk sets with strict `entry < t`; left truncation is distinct from right censoring. Independent truncation and event-free status before first observed performance are not verified from the supplied histories.
- **AJ:** implementation uses simultaneous monthly cause counts and the identified left-truncated risk set. The plotted and described curve is an estimate for the analyzed cohort. Population interpretation requires assumptions not established by this audit.
- **IPCW Brier:** score denominator includes all horizon-eligible loans, including pre-horizon censored records with zero contribution. The reverse-KM ratio uses left limits and delayed-entry risk sets. Administrative censoring is tied to calendar/FPD; near-unit weights do not establish censoring independence or unbiased full-cohort performance.
- **Known-status Brier:** separately calculated over observed status only; censoring strictly before the horizon is excluded, while censoring at the horizon is known through that reported month. It is not automatically representative of all eligible records.
- **Bootstrap:** 100 paired test-ID resamples for Cox-minus-AJ, with model predictions/reference fixed; percentile intervals are conditional on the fitted pipeline. The metrics bootstrap likewise does not refit the models or represent split/cohort uncertainty.
- **Calibration and discrimination:** C-index is ranking discrimination; Brier is squared probability prediction error; calibration summaries are descriptive predicted-versus-weighted-observed comparisons. Calibration summaries have no intervals.
- **Validation:** random split is within the 2015 vintage. Calendar split is internal and predominantly 2016 FPD, while training outcomes matured through 2026; it is not a historical deployment simulation or external validation.

## Assumptions and unresolved limits

Independent left truncation/entry and event-free status between FPD and first observed performance are not established. The monthly data cannot order primary and competing events recorded in the same month. Administrative censoring is associated with calendar entry/FPD, and a marginal reverse-KM does not demonstrate independent censoring conditional on the needed variables; consequently the IPCW score is assumption-dependent. The 2015 Freddie Mac sample does not provide external validation. Calibration has no intervals, bootstrap conditions on fitted models/predictions and does not include model fitting, split, or cohort uncertainty. PH diagnostics are approximate screening diagnostics; the supplied runtime does not support `compute_residuals()` for an entry-column fit. These are stated limitations, not resolved empirical assumptions.
